Resolution No. SI-R26-018: Act Authorizing Persons Entitled to Receive Information Regarding Trust Funds Managed by the Bureau of Trust Funds Administration for the Pojoaque Basin Regional Water System

At a duly called meeting of the Council of San Ildefonso Pueblo on the 15th day of October, 2026, the following resolution was adopted:

WHEREAS, the Pueblo de San Ildefonso ("Pueblo") is one of the four federally recognized sovereign Indian tribes, along with the Pueblo of Nambé, the Pueblo of Pojoaque and the Pueblo of Tesuque, which comprise the Northern Pueblos Tributary Water Rights Association ("the Association"); and,

WHEREAS, the four Pueblos are parties to the settlement of the long-running general stream adjudication of the Rio Nambė-Rio Tesuque-Rio Pojoaque stream system in northern New Mexico, captioned New Mexico ex rel. State Engineer v. Aamodt (the "Aamodt case"), and they are stakeholders in the Pojoaque Basin Regional Water System (the "System"), being constructed as the keystone of the settlement; and,

WHEREAS, the Bureau of Trust Funds Administration of the United States Department of the Interior ("BTFA") is holding and managing two funds created by Congress in its approval of the Aamodt settlement, the "BOR Fund," consisting of moneys intended to be used to pay operation, maintenance and repair costs of the System, prior to its conveyance to the four Pueblos and Santa Fe County, and the "Pueblo O, M & R Fund," consisting of moneys intended to be used to help pay the four Pueblos' shares of operation, maintenance and repair costs of the System, after it has been conveyed to the four Pueblos and Santa Fe County (collectively, the "Funds"); and,

WHEREAS, it is important that authorized representatives of the four Pueblos and the Association be able to obtain regular information from BTFA regarding the management and investment of these Funds, and pursuant to policies of the BTFA the Association wishes to specify the persons entitled to receive such information; and,

WHEREAS, the Association has passed a resolution that identifies representatives of each of the Pueblos who would be entitled to receive information concerning the management and investment of the Funds; and,

WHEREAS, each Pueblo must authorize that those identified representatives are entitled to receive information regarding the Funds on behalf of the Pueblo as part of the Association as well as the Association.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Pueblo de San Ildefonso enacts the "ACT AUTHORIZING PERSONS ENTITLED TO RECEIVE INFORMATION REGARDING TRUST FUNDS MANAGED BY THE BUREAU OF TRUST FUNDS ADMINISTRATION FOR THE POJOAQUE BASIN REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM"

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing resolution was duly voted upon by the Council on October 15, 2026, at a meeting with a vote of 8 in favor, 0 opposed, 1 abstaining and 1 absent (Governor presiding and not voting) pursuant to the authority vested in the Council by the Agreement of the Pueblo de San Ildefonso.

Denise Moquino, Council Secretary

ACT AUTHORIZING PERSONS ENTITLED TO RECEIVE INFORMATION REGARDING TRUST FUNDS MANAGED BY THE BUREAU OF TRUST FUNDS ADMINISTRATION FOR THE POJOAQUE BASIN REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM

New Section 11.2.30.010

Section 1.1. Short Title

This enactment shall be known as the "ACT AUTHORIZING PERSONS ENTITLED TO RECEIVE INFORMATION REGARDING TRUST FUNDS MANAGED BY THE BUREAU OF TRUST FUNDS ADMINISTRATION FOR THE POJOAQUE BASIN REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM" ("Act").

New Section 11.2.30.020

Section 1.2. Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to identify and authorize the persons who represent the Pueblo de San Ildefonso on the Northern Pueblo Tributary Water Rights Association ("the Association") who will be entitled to receive information regarding trust funds that are managed by the Bureau of Trust Funds Administration ("BTFA") for the Pojoaque Basin Regional Water System as part of the Aamodt water rights settlement.

New Section 11.2.30.030

Section 1.3. Findings

The Council hereby finds and declares that:

(a) the Pueblo de San Ildefonso ("Pueblo") is one of the four federally recognized sovereign Indian tribes, along with the Pueblo of Nambé, the Pueblo of Pojoaque and the Pueblo of Tesuque, which comprise the Northern Pueblos Tributary Water Rights Association ("the Association").

(b) the four Pueblos are parties to the settlement of the long-running general stream adjudication of the Rio Nambé-Rio Tesuque-Rio Pojoaque stream system in northern New Mexico, captioned New Mexico ex rel. State Engineer v. Aamodt (the "Aamodt case"), and they are stakeholders in the Pojoaque Basin Regional Water System (the "System"), being constructed as the keystone of the settlement.

(c) the Bureau of Trust Funds Administration of the United States Department of the Interior ("BTFA") is holding and managing two funds created by Congress in its approval of the Aamodt settlement, the "BOR Fund," consisting of moneys intended to be used to pay operation, maintenance and repair costs of the System, prior to its conveyance to the four Pueblos and Santa Fe County, and the "Pueblo O, M & R Fund," consisting of moneys intended to be used to help pay the four Pueblos' shares of operation, maintenance and repair costs of the System, after it has been conveyed to the four Pueblos and Santa Fe County (collectively, the "Funds").

(d) it is important that authorized representatives of the four Pueblos and the Association be able to obtain regular information from BTFA regarding the management and investment of these Funds, and pursuant to policies of the BTFA the Association wishes to specify the persons entitled to receive such information.

(e) the Association has passed a resolution that identifies representatives of each of the Pueblos who would be entitled to receive information concerning the management and investment of the Funds.

(f) each Pueblo must authorize that those identified representatives are entitled to receive information regarding the Funds on behalf of the Pueblo as part of the Association as well as the Association.

New Section 11.2.30.040

Section 1.4. Authorizations

(a) The Council identifies the following persons entitled to receive information concerning management and investment of the Funds:

1. The Governor and Lieutenant Governor;

2. The duly elected officers of the Association;

3. The designated representatives of the Pueblo attending meetings of the Association;

4. Attorneys representing the Pueblo.

(b) Upon request of BTFA, the Governor will provide BTFA representatives with the names of the individuals entitled to receive information concerning the Funds as identified in 1.4(a) above.

(c) The Governor or his designee is authorized to take such actions as necessary to carry out the intent of this Act.
